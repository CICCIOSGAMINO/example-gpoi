GDPR - General Data Protection Regulation
=========================================
[TOC] 

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) it's a regulation in EU law on data protection and privacy active in the European Union and European Economic Area. It also addresses the transfer of personal data of EU citizens outside the EU and EEA areas. The GDPR aims primarily to give control to individuals over their personal data and to simplify the regulatory environment for international business by unifying the regulation within the EU.


# Glossary

**EU**: European Union, a political and economic union of 27 member states located primarily in Europe.

**EEA**: European Economic Area, an area that provides for the free movement of persons, goods, services, and capital within the European Single Market, which includes the EU member states and three of the four EFTA member states (Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway).

**EU law**: The body of laws and regulations that govern the European Union and its member states.

**EU regulation**: Is a binding legislative act of the European Union that becomes immediately enforceable as law in all member states simultaneously. It must be applied in its entirety across the EU without the need for national governments to pass any enabling legislation.

**EU directive**: It a legislative act that set out a goal that EU countries must achive. However, it is up to the individual countries to devise their own laws on how to reach these goals.

[EU single-use plastics directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32019L0904&qid=1664779252884)

**EU decision**: A "decision" is a binding legislative act that is applicable to specific individuals, companies, or member states. It is directly applicable and does not require national implementation.

[EU decision on Croatia adoption to the Euro](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32022D1211&qid=1664779252884)

**EU recommendation**: A non-binding legislative act that allows the institutions to make their views known and to suggest a line of action without imposing any legal obligation on those to whom it is addressed.

[EU recommendation media sector](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32022H1634&qid=1664779252884)

**EU Opinion**: An "opinion" is an instrument that allows the institutions to make a statement in a non-binding fashion, in other words without imposing any legal obligation on those to whom it is addressed. An opinion is not binding. It can be issued by the main EU institutions (Commission, Council, Parliament), the Committee of the Regions and the European Economic and Social Committee. While laws are being made, the committees give opinions from their specific regional or economic and social viewpoint.

[EU opinion on enhancing swift delivery](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52021IE3668&qid=1664779252884)

**Data protection**: The process of safeguarding important information from corruption, compromise, or loss. In the context of GDPR, it refers to the protection of personal data of individuals.

**Privacy**: The right of individuals to keep their personal information secure and to control how it is collected, used, and shared.

**Personal data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (data subject). This can include names, identification numbers, location data, online identifiers, or factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural, or social identity of that person.
